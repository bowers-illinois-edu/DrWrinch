# Package index

## Bayes factors

The two Bayes factors from one model, under uniform or stated weights on
the shares, and for an archive of known size.

- [`bf_uniform_weights()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/bf_uniform_weights.md)
  : The uniform-weights Bayes factor
- [`bf_worst_case()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/bf_worst_case.md)
  : Worst-case Bayes factor: the rival at its most favorable share
- [`bf_rescaled()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/bf_rescaled.md)
  : The uniform-weights Bayes factor under weights the researcher states
- [`bf_uniform_weights_finite()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/bf_finite.md)
  [`bf_worst_case_finite()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/bf_finite.md)
  : Both Bayes factors for documents read from an archive of known size

## Sensitivity analysis

How biased the search, or how many observations re-coded, it would take
before the paper’s rule stops setting the rival aside.

- [`sens_bias()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/sens_bias.md)
  : Sensitivity to a biased search
- [`sens_coding()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/sens_coding.md)
  : Sensitivity to coding

## Interactive app

A Shiny app that shows both Bayes factors and the sensitivity analyses
in a browser.

- [`run_app()`](https://bowers-illinois-edu.github.io/DrWrinch/reference/run_app.md)
  : Launch the DrWrinch Shiny app
